FAQ

Common questions.

Quick answers for people just starting out.

Do I need a degree to start?

No. Many professionals are self-taught. A strong portfolio, hands-on lab experience and recognized certifications often matter more than a degree.

How long does the full roadmap take?

Around 12 months or more with consistent weekly practice. Your pace depends on how many hours you can give each week.

Is ethical hacking legal?

Yes, when you have explicit permission. Practice only on lab platforms, your own systems, or targets that authorize testing.

Which programming language should I learn first?

Start with Python for automation and tooling, and learn Bash for working comfortably in Linux.

Do I need an expensive computer?

No. A modest laptop with around 8 GB of RAM can run a Linux virtual machine, and many platforms offer browser-based labs.
