The Path

Six stages from beginner to advanced. Tap any stage to explore it.

01 Foundations+

Understand how computers and networks work.

Networking (TCP/IP, DNS, HTTP)Linux CLIWindows basicsPythonBash
Goal: Comfortable with the terminal and core protocols.

02 Security Basics+

Learn the language and principles of security.

CIA triadCryptographyThreat modelingIAMOWASP
Goal: Pass CompTIA Security+ level knowledge.

03 Ethical Hacking+

Think like an attacker, legally and ethically.

Recon & OSINTNmapMetasploitBurp SuiteWireshark
Goal: Complete beginner rooms on TryHackMe and earn eJPT.

04 Pentesting+

Test web apps, networks and Active Directory.

SQLi & XSSPrivilege escalationActive DirectoryHack The BoxReporting
Goal: Write professional reports; target PNPT or OSCP.

05 Defense & Forensics+

Detect, respond and investigate.

SIEMIncident responseMalware analysisDigital forensicsCloud security
Goal: Understand both attack and defense.

06 Advanced & Career+

Specialize and go professional.

Red teamingExploit developmentBug bountyCTFsReverse engineering
Goal: Pick a specialty and build a public portfolio.
